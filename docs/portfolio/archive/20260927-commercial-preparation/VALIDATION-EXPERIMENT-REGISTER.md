## Interview core

1. Describe the last time this problem occurred.
2. What triggered it, and what did you do?
3. What did it cost in time, money, risk, or missed opportunity?
4. What do you use now, and what fails?
5. Who decides and who pays?
6. What would make a trial safe enough?
7. Ask for a real next step; do not ask whether the idea sounds good.

## Outreach templates

**Problem interview:** “I’m researching how [specific role] currently handles [specific job]. I’m not asking you to buy anything. Could I ask about the last real instance and what made it difficult? Twenty minutes; no confidential material is needed.”

**Design partner:** “Based on the workflow you described, I can run a bounded [product] trial using [exact input] to produce [exact output]. The trial will state its limits, collect only agreed evidence, and can be stopped at any time. Would you like a one-page scope?”
