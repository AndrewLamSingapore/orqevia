# Six Product Launch Packages

Status: **INTERNAL PACKAGE READY FOR OWNER REVIEW; NOT EXTERNALLY VALIDATED**

## 1. JARVIS PRIME

**Promise:** Complete one evidence-heavy workflow with governed identity, authority, and receipts—not unsupported “general autonomy.”  
**Demo:** Owner submits a harmless objective; PRIME shows selected agent/runtime, returns Chinese or English result and artifact, and preserves authoritative metadata.  
**Trial:** One workflow, one Owner, bounded Members, two weeks, no public runtime exposure.  
**Success:** Customer uses it for a real workflow twice and asks to keep or expand it.  
**Failure:** Interest without a real objective, or results cannot outperform the customer’s current process.  
**FAQ boundary:** Private runtime, scoped integrations, no claim of infallibility, audio excluded until separately accepted.

## 2. VELYQUA

**Promise:** A clearer, durable picture of a living-water system from manual observations and existing tools before custom hardware is required.  
**Demo:** Create a tank, establish baseline, record test/care event, inspect Aqua Now and Tank Memory, export records.  
**Trial:** 30 days with one real tank using manual data; sensors optional and explicitly unverified.  
**Success:** Keeper records at least seven observations, returns weekly, and identifies one better-informed decision.  
**Failure:** Logging burden exceeds value or advice cannot be understood and checked.  
**FAQ boundary:** Not a substitute for professional aquatic/veterinary advice; simulation is labelled; no physical telemetry claim without measured evidence.

## 3. The Portal

**Promise:** Turn one encounter into a structured, inspectable artifact and a better experiment—not another generic summary.  
**Input:** A source, object, place, observation, question, or idea plus any citations the customer owns.  
**Output:** Artifact, provenance, connections, unresolved question, and experiment candidate/export.  
**Trial:** One facilitated 60–90 minute Artifact Foundry session.  
**Success:** User exports or shares the artifact and uses the proposed question/experiment.  
**Failure:** Connections are merely decorative or cannot change the next decision.  
**FAQ boundary:** Generated curation is a discovery aid, not historical authority.

## 4. The Sky Tablet

**Promise:** Give visitors an atmospheric, evidence-aware cultural-history encounter that can be adapted without rebuilding a full app.  
**Demo:** Complete the city-to-sky journey, use free exploration, open Evidence Lens.  
**Trial:** Moderated demo plus a scoped content-adaptation concept for one programme.  
**Success:** Buyer requests a brief, content adaptation, or pilot quote; visitors complete and accurately recall the intended learning point.  
**Failure:** Experience is beautiful but does not support a programme objective.  
**FAQ boundary:** Interpretive reconstruction; illustrative planet positions; no archaeological-scan claim.

## 5. Game Platform / Living Worlds

**Promise:** Persistent AI-assisted worlds whose consequences survive because authoritative state is structured rather than entrusted to model prose.  
**Developer demo:** Send player intent and bounded world state; receive narration, state changes, NPC actions, checks, choices, memory updates, and GM notes as structured JSON.  
**Player demo:** Create a guest campaign, complete a consequential turn, close, return, and see the consequence persist.  
**Trial:** One developer sandbox scenario or three-session player test.  
**Success:** Developer integrates the contract or player voluntarily returns.  
**Failure:** Structured control adds friction without improving continuity or safety.  
**FAQ boundary:** Temporary guest identity is browser-bound; no unsupported claim of limitless worlds.

## 6. Authority Engine

**Promise:** Replace “trust me” with a reviewer-ready evidence package that separates story, source, verification, and limits.  
**Input:** A small set of material claims and original evidence.  
**Output:** Claim/evidence map, provenance boundaries, hashes/receipts where appropriate, visible limitations, and independent-review instructions.  
**Trial:** One consequential claim packaged manually as a fixed-scope design-partner service.  
**Success:** An independent reviewer verifies the claim faster and identifies no hidden authority substitution.  
**Failure:** Package adds ceremony without improving review speed or confidence.  
**FAQ boundary:** Evidence presentation is not legal certification, audit opinion, or proof of commercial success.

## Common launch assets

- Contact path: product-specific query routed through Authority Engine contact with source and intent parameters.
- Feedback capture: experiment ID, product ID, consent state, participant type, problem statement, observed use, result, follow-up permission.
- Support: bounded founder-led support during design-partner experiments; response target is a hypothesis until exercised.
- Analytics: collect only events required to measure activation and return usage; exclude private objective/content payloads by default.
- Terms: pilot scope, acceptable use, data purpose, retention, limitations, deletion/contact route; professional review required before binding use.
