# Privacy and Legal Readiness Register

Status: **PRELIMINARY OPERATIONAL READINESS / NOT LEGAL ADVICE / PROFESSIONAL REVIEW PENDING**

## Portfolio controls before external collection

- Record purpose and consent basis per experiment.
- Collect the minimum identity/contact information needed to schedule, support, and follow up.
- Keep interview notes and product telemetry separate from authentication secrets and production data.
- Do not collect passwords, private keys, government identifiers, health data, children's data, or unnecessary confidential business data.
- Define retention at experiment start; default hypothesis is 90 days for raw interview/usage data and longer only for de-identified evidence receipts.
- Provide access, correction, withdrawal, and deletion contact instructions.
- Restrict access to Owner-authorized operators; preserve audit evidence without exposing participant content.
- Record processors, hosting region, and cross-border transfers before collection.
- Maintain a breach-response contact and evidence-preserving incident process.
- Obtain professional Singapore legal review before binding terms, paid launch, sensitive-data processing, or claims of PDPA compliance.
