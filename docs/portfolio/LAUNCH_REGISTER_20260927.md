## Before execution

- Start with the participant's last real problem, current process and cost; show
  the demo after understanding the workflow. Do not ask only whether an idea
  sounds attractive.
- Record experiment/product IDs, time, participant class, consent/input rights,
  direct observation, source receipt, limitations and follow-up permission. Quote
  only with permission; retain digests when evidence is stored as a file.
- A technical trial needs real users, not invented respondents or agent-generated
  testimonials. No contact, payment, advertisement or commitment occurred in this
  reconciliation.
- Use minimum necessary data; exclude secrets, confidential third-party content
  and unnecessary identity/device data. Existing 90-day retention is only a
  hypothesis pending the specific experiment's decision.
- Existing authority to prepare/operate does not require another routine planning
  round. Missing real participant access/consent, rights or binding business/legal
  decisions remain genuine gates; do not invent them as software dependencies.
- Preserve six independent products. Authority Engine may provide an optional
  contact/evidence entry point; it is not mandatory infrastructure for the other
  products. Shared Evidence Lens claims must not exceed executed qualification.
